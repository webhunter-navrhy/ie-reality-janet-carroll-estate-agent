PROPERTY DESCRIPTION

Janet Carroll Estate Agents proudly presents Apartment 30, a stunning B energy rated duplex penthouse in Carysfort Hall, now available for sale by Private Treaty.

Extending to approximately 161.5 sq. m. (1,738 sq. ft.), this impressive home was constructed around 1993 and stands out as the premier unit within the complex thanks to its optimal orientation. Positioned on the southwest wing, it benefits from two generous southwest-facing terraces, superb balconies and additional gated parking, all contributing to its abundance of natural light and appeal.

Set within the tranquil, secluded grounds of Carysfort Hall, Apartment 30 offers a luxurious living experience in a truly exquisite environment. Overlooking picturesque Carysfort Park and the UCD Michael Smurfit Graduate Business School, the development enjoys a low-density, mature and beautifully landscaped setting, meticulously managed for residents` comfort.

One of the standout features of this penthouse is its dual aspect, allowing residents to savour breakfast in the morning sun on one terrace and enjoy the evening glow as the sunset graces Carysfort Park from the other.

Nestled within a private gated development, Apartment 30 is within walking distance of charming Blackrock Village and enjoys a highly coveted Blackrock address. Residents benefit from easy access to an excellent range of amenities close by, including schools, colleges, shops, restaurants, cafés, parks, playgrounds and sports facilities.

Transport options are plentiful, with the DART, quality bus corridors, cycle routes and walking tracks all nearby, ensuring convenient connectivity. Residents can also enjoy leisurely strolls along the seafront and access two recently renovated shopping centres offering a superb standard of retail therapy.

This exceptional property caters to a diverse range of lifestyles, making it an ideal choice for first-time buyers, downsizers, or those seeking to enhance their quality of life within a well-maintained, safe and gated community. Investors should also take note of the excellent opportunity this property presents never previously rented, it offers strong potential for medium- to long-term rental income in the highly desirable Blackrock area.
